What cleaning services do you offer in Calgary?

Office cleaning, janitorial, carpet, floor and window cleaning, plus industry-specific cleaning including healthcare and hospitality.

How experienced is your staff?

Many of our team members have years of commercial cleaning experience, and every crew member is trained in green cleaning.

Can you tailor the cleaning schedule?

Yes. Cleaning can be set up daily, weekly, bi-weekly or monthly to suit your business.

What products and equipment do you use?

Environmentally friendly products and modern equipment that is serviced regularly.

What sets Regency apart?

Eco-friendly practices, trained staff, quality control, a strong customer focus and flexible scheduling.

Can you work around our business hours?

Yes. Services can be booked around your hours, including evenings and weekends.

Corporate offices, warehouses, healthcare clinics, retail stores, condominiums and similar facilities.

Yes. We aim to use environmentally responsible, non-toxic solutions.

Yes. Plans are tailored to your industry, facility size and business needs.

Our service guarantee means scheduled cleanings are not missed.

Yes. We prepare spaces for occupancy or reopening after construction or renovation.
